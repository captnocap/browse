# Angle 04 — Regulation / Policy

## Claims (with confidence)

- Claim (high): EU AI Act's Aug 2, 2026 deadline is the single biggest regulatory event -- full high-risk system requirements, conformity assessments, and penalties up to 35M EUR / 7% global revenue kick in.
- Claim (high): US has no federal AI law. Trump's Dec 2025 executive order attempts to preempt state laws but lacks legal force to override them -- only Congress/courts can do that.
- Claim (high): States are filling the US vacuum. Colorado AI Act (June 30, 2026) is first comprehensive US high-risk AI statute. California has ~6 new AI laws effective 2026 (training data transparency, companion chatbot safety, healthcare AI disclosure).
- Claim (medium): No jurisdiction has agent-specific regulation yet. Current laws treat AI agents under existing frameworks (product liability, high-risk AI systems). The EU Product Liability Directive (implementation by Dec 2026) explicitly covers software/AI as "products."
- Claim (medium): China's amended Cybersecurity Law (Jan 1, 2026) adds AI security reviews; penalties reach 50M CNY / 5% turnover. Draft comprehensive AI law still pending.
- Claim (high): UK and Japan are deliberately light-touch. UK uses sector-by-sector guidance, no binding AI law. Japan's 2025 AI Promotion Act is non-binding/voluntary.
- Claim (low): No major enforcement fines have been issued yet under AI-specific statutes as of early 2026 -- the big deadlines are mid-to-late 2026.

## Evidence

- EU AI Act Aug 2026: high-risk obligations, transparency Art. 50, mandatory regulatory sandboxes per member state ([EU AI Act timeline](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/timeline/timeline-implementation-eu-ai-act))
- Trump EO Dec 11, 2025 directs AG to create task force challenging state AI laws; Commerce must ID "burdensome" state laws by Mar 2026 ([King & Spalding analysis](https://www.kslaw.com/news-and-insights/new-state-ai-laws-are-effective-on-january-1-2026-but-a-new-executive-order-signals-disruption))
- Colorado AI Act delayed to June 30, 2026; requires impact assessments, anti-discrimination measures ([Wilson Sonsini preview](https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/2026-year-in-preview-ai-regulatory-developments-for-companies-to-watch-out-for.html))
- EU Product Liability Directive includes AI/software as "products," strict liability for defects, implementation deadline Dec 2026 ([Squire Patton Boggs](https://www.squirepattonboggs.com/insights/publications/the-agentic-ai-revolution-managing-legal-risks/))
- China Cybersecurity Law amended Jan 2026 with AI security reviews ([Sumsub global tracker](https://sumsub.com/blog/comprehensive-guide-to-ai-laws-and-regulations-worldwide/))
- Japan AI Promotion Act (May 2025) is non-binding, voluntary framework ([FPF analysis](https://fpf.org/blog/understanding-japans-ai-promotion-act-an-innovation-first-blueprint-for-ai-regulation/))

## What I'm unsure about

- Whether any EU member state has actually stood up its mandatory AI regulatory sandbox yet
- Exact scope of Trump EO preemption -- legal challenges are likely but no rulings yet
- Whether China's draft comprehensive AI law will pass in 2026
- How existing product liability frameworks will actually apply to multi-agent chains (developer vs. deployer vs. orchestrator liability)
