# Angle 07 — Key Players & Incentives

## Claims (with confidence)

- **OpenAI dominates consumer reach** (high): ChatGPT holds ~80% of gen-AI traffic. Operator (CUA-powered browser agent) shipped Jan 2025, now integrating into unified ChatGPT agent. $40B raise in 2025 funds compute moat.
- **Anthropic targets prosumer/developer niche** (high): Claude Cowork (Jan 2026) brings agentic desktop automation to non-technical users. MCP donated to open Agentic AI Foundation, becoming a de facto interop standard. Opus 4.6 leads agentic benchmarks.
- **Google bets on universal agents** (medium): Project Mariner (browser) + Project Astra (multimodal/physical world) aim to merge into a single cross-device agent by late 2026. Apple chose Google over Anthropic/OpenAI for its core AI partnership.
- **Apple is a platform kingmaker, not a model builder** (high): Xcode 26.3 integrates Claude Agent SDK + OpenAI Codex. Apple picks partners, taxes distribution, avoids model training costs.
- **Open-source frameworks are the real infrastructure layer** (medium): LangGraph dominates production agentic apps; CrewAI leads multi-agent orchestration; browser-use is SOTA open browser automation. AutoGPT has 167K GitHub stars but ceded production mindshare.
- **Capital concentration is extreme and accelerating** (high): OpenAI ($40B), Anthropic ($13B), xAI ($10B) absorbed most 2025 AI funding. Startup agent market grew to $7.8B but VC is flowing to a handful of category leaders.
- **Closed vs open is a false binary** (medium): Open models provide negotiating leverage and portability. Governments back open AI (DeepSeek, Qwen) as strategic assets. But closed APIs still win on speed-to-production.

- Meta's consumer agent strategy — surprisingly quiet despite LLaMA dominance in open weights
