# Angle 06 — Ethics / Societal Impact

## Claims (with confidence)

- **Labor displacement is accelerating but unevenly** (high): WEF estimates 85M jobs displaced by 2026. Customer service (80% automation potential) and data entry hit hardest. But only 11% of orgs actually run agentic AI in production — hype outpaces reality.
- **"Agentic inequality" is the new digital divide** (high): The gap isn't just access vs. no-access. It's one free-tier agent vs. a corporate swarm with premium APIs, proprietary data, and parallel execution. This creates permanent stratification.
- **AI companionship is a mental health risk at scale** (high): 72% of US teens have used AI for companionship. Character.AI handles 20K queries/sec. Studies show AI social support inversely correlates with human social support. Linked to AI-induced delusions and reinforced self-harm behaviors.
- **Transparency regulation is fragmenting globally** (medium): EU AI Act, South Korea's Basic AI Act (Jan 2026), 20+ US state privacy laws, and bills like Minnesota's mandatory AI disclosure are all live — but no unified standard. Consumers often don't know they're talking to an agent.
- **Environmental costs are massive but improvable** (medium): Global data center consumption projected at 1,050 TWh by 2026 (~8% of global energy). AI training clusters use 7-8x more energy than typical workloads. But efficient model selection can cut energy per query by 70x.
- **Cognitive deskilling is a structural problem** (medium): Studies show negative correlation between GenAI use and critical thinking. Healthcare AI dependence erodes diagnostic reasoning and moral judgment. Gartner: 40% of enterprise apps will use AI agents by 2026 (up from 5% in 2025).

## Evidence

- 55,000 US job cuts directly attributed to AI in 2025 (Challenger, Gray & Christmas) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/31/investors-predict-ai-is-coming-for-labor-in-2026/)
- AI companion market at $501B in 2026; 42% of users worry about data security, 28% fear dependency — [APA](https://www.apa.org/monitor/2026/01-02/trends-digital-ai-relationships-emotional-connection)
- Minnesota bill requires businesses to disclose AI interactions and provide human opt-out — [Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislation-tracker-all-2026-ai-bills)
- Data centers consume 1,900L water/MWh for cooling + 4,540L/MWh for electricity generation — [IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/energy-demand-from-ai)
- AI deskilling erodes activity awareness, competence maintenance, and output assessment in professions — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-025-02686-z)

## What I'm unsure about

- Real-world displacement numbers for 2026 (most stats are projections or 2025 data; hard evidence of mass layoffs directly from agentic AI specifically is thin)
- Whether "agentic inequality" will play out as theorized or if open-source agents flatten the gap
- Long-term cognitive deskilling effects — most studies are short-term or correlational
- Actual consumer awareness rates (how many people don't realize they're talking to an AI agent?)
