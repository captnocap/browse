# Angle 01 — What Changed (Last 6-12 Months)

## Claims (with confidence)

- **Computer-using agents shipped from all three major labs** (high): OpenAI launched Operator (Jan 2025), Anthropic shipped Computer Use + Cowork, and Google launched Chrome Auto Browse (Jan 2026). The browser/desktop agent went from demo to product in under 12 months.
- **Manus went from viral demo to $2B Meta acquisition in 9 months** (high): Chinese startup Butterfly Effect launched Manus in March 2025, hit 2M waitlist signups in a week, raised $75M at $500M valuation, then was acquired by Meta for $2B by end of 2025.
- **ChatGPT roughly doubled to ~900M weekly active users** (medium): OpenAI reported ~400M WAU in early 2025, growing to ~900M by Dec 2025, approaching the 1B milestone expected Q1 2026.
- **AI wrapper startups are dying at scale** (high): Series A shutdowns up 2.5x YoY. Builder.ai ($1.2B valuation, Microsoft-backed) went bankrupt mid-2025. Gartner says only ~130 of thousands of "agentic AI" vendors are real; the rest are agent-washing.
- **Enterprise agent adoption is still early despite the hype** (high): Only 11% of orgs have agents in production (Deloitte). Gartner predicts 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027. Best models complete <25% of real-world tasks on first attempt (APEX benchmark).
- **ai.com launched as a new consumer agent platform** (medium): Crypto.com CEO Kris Marszalek debuted ai.com during Super Bowl 60 (Feb 2026) with autonomous agents for scheduling, shopping, and messaging. Too early to gauge traction.

## Evidence

- Google Chrome Auto Browse: Gemini 3-powered, $19.99/mo (20 tasks/day) or $249.99/mo (200 tasks/day), US-only at launch ([Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/chrome/gemini-3-auto-browse/))
- Anthropic released Opus 4.5 (Nov 2025) then Opus 4.6 (Feb 2026) with "agent teams"; launched Cowork for non-coder agent use on macOS ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/05/anthropic-releases-opus-4-6-with-new-agent-teams/))
- OpenAI launched Operator (Jan 2025) powered by CUA model, later integrated into ChatGPT as "ChatGPT agent"; launched OpenAI Frontier for enterprise agent management (Feb 2026) ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-operator/))
- Manus viral demo: 1M+ views, 2M waitlist, Benchmark-led $75M round, then Meta acquisition for $2B ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manus_(AI_agent)))
- ChatGPT: ~900M WAU, ~200M DAU, 2.5B daily prompts as of late 2025 ([DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/chatgpt-statistics/))
- AI agents market: $7.6B (2025) projected to $10.9B (2026), 45.8% CAGR through 2030 ([Warmly](https://www.warmly.ai/p/blog/ai-agents-statistics))
- 42% of companies abandoned most AI initiatives in 2025, up from 17% in 2024 ([Medium](https://medium.com/utopian/why-90-of-ai-agent-startups-are-failing-92b86cb98af5))

## What I'm unsure about

- Exact ChatGPT WAU figures vary significantly across sources (800M vs 900M vs "approaching 1B") -- likely a mix of different measurement dates
- Whether Manus acquisition by Meta is fully closed or still pending regulatory review
- How many actual paying consumers are using Operator/Auto Browse/Cowork vs. just the headline launch announcements
- Gemini 3 vs Gemini 2.5 timeline is muddled across sources; exact release dates are inconsistent
- ai.com's actual technical capabilities vs. marketing claims (Super Bowl launch = heavy marketing spend, light evidence of product depth)
