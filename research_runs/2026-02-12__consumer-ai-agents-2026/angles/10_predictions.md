# Angle 10 — Predictions (Short Horizon, Justified)

## Claims (with confidence)

- **Agent-commerce goes live** (high): Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Google have already launched agent-purchase protocols. Consumer-facing agent shopping (low-stakes, high-frequency purchases) will be routine by Q4 2026. [6-9 months]
- **40%+ of agentic AI projects get cancelled** (high): Gartner-aligned estimates say unanticipated cost and scaling complexity will kill nearly half of current enterprise agent projects before 2027. Consumer wrappers die faster. [12 months]
- **Thin-wrapper startups collapse** (high): Startups whose moat is a prompt + UI over a foundation model face existential pressure as OpenAI/Google/Anthropic ship native agent features. CIOs already rationalizing vendor sprawl. [6-12 months]
- **Agent costs drop 10-15x** (medium): o3-mini already matched o1 at 15x lower cost. Inference cost deflation continues, making $20/mo consumer agents viable at scale. But orchestration/infra costs remain high. [6-12 months]
- **Colorado AI Act enforcement begins** (high): Takes effect June 30, 2026. First US law requiring impact assessments and algorithmic discrimination audits for AI deployers. Federal preemption fight will slow but not stop it. [5 months]
- **EU AI Act first compliance wave** (high): August 2, 2026 deadline for first-wave compliance. Will force labeling and transparency changes on consumer-facing agents sold in EU. [6 months]
- **AI task autonomy hits 8+ hours** (medium): METR data shows AI task duration doubling every 7 months. By late 2026, agents execute full-workday autonomous workflows. Consumer impact: real delegation, not just chat. [10 months]
- **No full "bubble pop"** (medium): Skeptics (Grantham) predict 50% market crash; optimists say fundamentals differ from dot-com. Most likely: a correction in AI startup valuations, not infrastructure. Revenue-generating agents survive. [12 months]
- **Self-verification solves error cascades** (low): The core reliability problem (errors compounding in multi-step agent tasks) has promising research solutions, but production-grade self-verification in consumer products is unlikely before mid-2027. [12-18 months]

## What I'm unsure about

- Whether federal preemption actually blocks state AI enforcement or just creates legal limbo
- How fast self-verification tech moves from research to shipped consumer products
- Whether the "correction" hits agent infra companies or just the thin-wrapper layer
- Prediction market liquidity on agent-specific outcomes is thin; most bets are on AGI timelines, not consumer product survival
