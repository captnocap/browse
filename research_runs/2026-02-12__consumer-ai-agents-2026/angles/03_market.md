# Angle 03 — Market / Business Reality

## Claims (with confidence)

- **The AI agent market is real but enterprise-dominated** (high): Global AI agents market ~$10.9B in 2026, growing at 43% CAGR through 2030. But enterprise spend dwarfs consumer.
- **OpenAI is running away with revenue** (high): OpenAI hit $20B ARR in 2025, tripling YoY. Projects ~$30B in 2026. No other consumer AI company is close.
- **ChatGPT dominates but share is eroding** (high): ChatGPT market share dropped from 87% to ~64-68% in 12 months. Gemini surged to 18-21%. Claude holds ~3.5% but growing 190% YoY.
- **Pricing is shifting from subscriptions to hybrid/outcome models** (medium): Pure subscription ($20/mo consumer, $200+/mo pro) is giving way to per-task and outcome-based pricing, especially for agentic features.
- **Most agent deployments are still pilot-stage** (high): Gartner says 40% of enterprise apps will have task-specific agents by end of 2026, up from <5% in 2025. But fewer than 1-in-4 orgs have scaled agents to production.
- **Consumer willingness outpaces actual adoption** (medium): 44% of US consumers say they'd use an AI agent as a personal assistant (70% Gen Z), but real daily usage remains concentrated in chat interfaces, not autonomous agents.

## Evidence

- OpenAI ARR tripled to $20B in 2025; hit first $1B revenue month in July 2025 ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/openais-annual-recurring-revenue-tripled-to-20-billion-in-2025/))
- Anthropic projected $2.2B revenue in 2025, 159% growth ([GPTrends](https://gptrends.io/blog/mid-2025-ai-chatbot-scorecard/))
- ChatGPT: 800M weekly active users; Gemini: 450M MAU; Claude: ~3.5% share but fastest enterprise growth ([SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/chatgpt-vs-google-gemini-statistics/), [Vertu](https://vertu.com/lifestyle/ai-chatbot-market-share-2026-chatgpt-drops-to-68-as-google-gemini-surges-to-18-2/))
- AI agents market $10.9B in 2026, projected $48.3B by 2030 at 43.3% CAGR ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/05/3213141/0/en/AI-Agents-Market-to-Grow-43-3-Annually-Through-2030.html))
- Gartner: 40% of enterprise apps will feature agents by 2026, up from <5% ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-08-26-gartner-predicts-40-percent-of-enterprise-apps-will-feature-task-specific-ai-agents-by-2026-up-from-less-than-5-percent-in-2025))
- Consumer pricing: $20/mo (ChatGPT Plus), $200/mo (ChatGPT Pro), per-task emerging via Zapier-style models ([Chargebee](https://www.chargebee.com/blog/pricing-ai-agents-playbook/))

## What I'm unsure about

- Actual consumer-only revenue breakdowns (OpenAI/Anthropic don't separate consumer vs enterprise revenue publicly)
- Whether per-task pricing will work for consumers or remain enterprise-only
- Real daily active user counts for autonomous agent features (vs. plain chat)
- How much of Gemini's MAU surge is organic vs. bundled into Google products
